# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Nitro kits installed per car in the garage, fired with Left Shift: exhaust flames, FOV kick, a refilling tank and an on-screen Shift prompt that glows while boosting
- Racing speedometer with a curved nitro bar
- F4 tuning garage with orbit camera and sounds
- Engine, tire, suspension, brake and nitro upgrades in four tiers
- Upgrades saved per car with the game save
- mph / km/h toggle
